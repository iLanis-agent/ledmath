LED Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out series resistors, chain drops and rail draw from the numbers you type. The voltages, currents and counts are yours to measure or read off your own parts. It treats every LED and resistor as ideal, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the bin, the temperature, the wiring or the supply, and it gives no verdict that a resistor, an LED or a rail is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The resistor truth

The series resistor for one LED. Voltages in V, current in mA.

The chain truth

Several LEDs in series drop their voltages together, and the resistor takes what is left.

The strings truth

Parallel strings each draw their own current, and the rail supplies the sum.
